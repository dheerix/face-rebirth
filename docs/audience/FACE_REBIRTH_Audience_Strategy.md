# Phase 1 (First 50 Clients)

## Primary Focus

1.  **Housewives / Mothers**
2.  **Makeup Artists & Beauty Professionals**
3.  **Women Aged 40--50** (across both groups)

### Why?

-   Available during the current session slots.
-   Strong interest in natural facial wellness.
-   High word-of-mouth potential.
-   Likely to generate authentic testimonials.

# Referral Flywheel

## Homemaker Path

→ Housing Society / Kitty Groups

Face Rebirth™ Consultation → 5--20 Referrals

# Strategic Positioning

Instead of marketing by profession, position Face Rebirth™ around life
stages and motivations:

-   Busy Women
-   Midlife Women (40--50)
-   Beauty Professionals (Clients + Referral Partners)
