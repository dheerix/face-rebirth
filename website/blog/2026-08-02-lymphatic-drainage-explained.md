The lymphatic system helps return excess tissue fluid to circulation and supports immune function. Unlike the heart-driven blood system, it relies partly on breathing, muscle activity, and body movement.

{/* truncate */}

## Why “gentle” matters

Many lymphatic vessels sit close to the skin. This is one reason lymphatic techniques are typically light and directional rather than deep and aggressive.

## What supports normal flow?

Regular walking, comfortable mobility, diaphragmatic breathing, hydration appropriate to your needs, and restorative sleep can all form part of a supportive routine.

Manual lymphatic drainage is also a specialized clinical technique. If you have swelling, lymph-node removal, heart or kidney concerns, infection, or another medical condition, ask your healthcare professional what is appropriate for you.
