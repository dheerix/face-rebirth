Stress is not only a thought. It can change breathing, sleep, muscle tone, posture, and everyday habits—and those shifts may become visible in the face.

{/* truncate */}

## The tension pattern

Some people press the tongue, clench the jaw, raise the shoulders, narrow the eyes, or hold the breath when concentrating. Repeated often enough, these become familiar resting patterns.

## Interrupting the loop

Try a small check-in: let your teeth separate, soften your tongue, exhale longer than you inhale, and allow your shoulders to drop. The goal is not to force relaxation; it is to give the system another option.

Jaw pain, headaches, tooth wear, or disrupted sleep may need assessment from a dentist, physician, physiotherapist, or other qualified professional.
