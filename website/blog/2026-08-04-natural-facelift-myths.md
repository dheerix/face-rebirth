“Natural facelift” is compelling language, but it can blur the difference between temporary changes, healthy rituals, and medical procedures. A facial practice may help you feel less tense or look more rested without literally reproducing surgery.

{/* truncate */}

## Myth: harder is always better

Facial tissue is not a problem to overpower. Forceful pulling, scraping, or repetitive exercise can cause irritation or discomfort. Comfort and consistency are more useful guides than intensity.

## Myth: one tool changes facial structure overnight

Short-term changes in color or puffiness are not the same as permanent structural change. Be cautious with dramatic before-and-after images made under different lighting, angles, or expressions.

## A better promise

A grounded practice can offer time for self-attention, mobility, relaxation, and noticing. Those benefits are worthwhile without inflated claims.
