Jaw tension can influence how your face feels and the expression you carry, but it is only one part of a much bigger ageing picture that includes genetics, sun exposure, health, hormones, sleep, and time.

{/* truncate */}

## What tension can change

Clenching may contribute to muscle fatigue, tenderness, headaches, tooth wear, and a sense of tightness around the jaw and temples. It may also make a strained expression feel like your default.

## What a facial practice cannot promise

Relaxation practices do not stop biological ageing, reshape bone, or replace dental and medical care. Their value is in comfort, awareness, and sustainable self-care.

If clenching is frequent, painful, or happens during sleep, seek professional assessment. A qualified provider can check for dental damage, temporomandibular disorders, and other contributing factors.
